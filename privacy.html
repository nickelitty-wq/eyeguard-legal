Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

EyeGuard Screen Breaker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

EyeGuard does not collect personally identifiable information directly. Most app data is stored locally on your device.

This locally stored data may include:

  • Break history
  • Preferences
  • Streak data
  • Usage statistics

Subscription and Payment Data

EyeGuard uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat and Apple may collect certain information required to process payments, such as purchase history, device identifiers, and subscription status.

EyeGuard does not have access to your full payment details. All transactions are handled securely through the App Store.

You can review RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy here: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Permissions

EyeGuard may request permission to send notifications and use vibration features to provide reminders and feedback. These permissions can be managed in your device settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Most app data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Your Rights and Choices

You may control, update, or delete your locally stored data by managing the app settings or uninstalling the app.

For subscription-related data, you may manage your information through your Apple ID account settings.

Children’s Privacy

EyeGuard is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
